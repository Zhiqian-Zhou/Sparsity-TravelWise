\begin{abstract}
European railway operators run several thousand passenger services per day, of which roughly one in ten arrives more than five minutes late or is cancelled. Predicting which services will be disrupted -- before they leave the platform and again at every stop along the route -- would let operators pre-stage staff, warn passengers, and reroute around the rolling stock most likely to fail. We present a unified pipeline that does so across the very different open-data feeds of Italy, Finland and the Netherlands. The pipeline ingests \num{16586834} stop rows from three open sources, harmonises them under a single anti-leakage Common Data Model with 37 (pre-departure) or 40 (inflight) features, trains four model families on identical splits, and ties the resulting SHAP attributions back to a queryable knowledge graph. On the held-out June~2024 test set the strongest pre-departure model (XGBoost) reaches a PR-AUC of \num{0.552}; the inflight variant -- which additionally sees actual delays at prior stops -- reaches \num{0.952}, with a lag-feature ablation showing that almost the entire uplift is carried by one feature. Because cause-of-disruption labels exist only for the Netherlands, we additionally train five multi-class classifiers on \num{126810} Dutch labelled stops; a v2 redesign with cause-specific feature engineering and symmetric sample weighting lifts test macro-F1 by \SI{46}{\percent} (\num{0.146}~$\to$~\num{0.213}) and rescues the rare \emph{weather} class from F1$=$\num{0.000} to F1$=$\num{0.509}. We then transfer to Italy and Finland and document where the transfer fails: the model never predicts \emph{weather} on Finland despite Finland's harsher winter, a covariate-shift artefact we attribute to incompatible feature scales between Open-Meteo daily aggregates and FMI hourly observations. The full pipeline -- a 32-test suite, a Sparksee~5.2.3 knowledge graph driven from Python via JPype, and 51 inspection figures -- is reproducible from raw open data with three shell commands.
\end{abstract}
